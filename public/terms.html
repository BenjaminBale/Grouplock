2. What Grouple does

Grouple is a payment tool that lets a group of people split the cost of a booking and pay their own share individually. Grouple is not a booking platform: the underlying reservation (the villa, property, or service being paid for) is made directly with the Merchant, on the Merchant's own website or booking system. Grouple only handles the payment-splitting and collection process.

3. How payment works

  • When a Guest starts a group payment, each member of the group pays their share individually via Stripe.
  • Funds are held by Grouple until either (a) the full group has paid and the Merchant confirms the booking, or (b) the payment window expires or the Merchant declines.
  • If the group does not fully pay, or the Merchant declines to confirm within the agreed response window, everyone who has already paid is automatically refunded in full.
  • Once the Merchant confirms a fully-paid booking, the funds (minus Grouple's fee, see clause 5) are released to the Merchant, and the booking is treated as confirmed between the Guest and the Merchant.
