Grouple

Grouple is a payment method for group travel — like Apple Pay or Klarna, not a booking site.

A group splits the cost of a villa or holiday booking at checkout — each person pays their own share, simultaneously, and the booking only confirms once everyone has paid. No one carries the cost. No one chases anyone else for money. The reservation itself stays entirely on the villa company's own website.
