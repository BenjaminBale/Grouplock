3. Who we share it with

  • Stripe — processes all payments and holds the payment method data. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — sends transactional emails (invite links, receipts, confirmations) on our behalf.
  • The Merchant a Guest is paying — sees the booking status and who in the group has paid, but not payment card details.

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it with anyone else for marketing purposes.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you're based, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these, contact [support email].

6. Cookies

Grouple uses one essential cookie to keep Merchants logged into their dashboard. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Security

Payment data never touches our servers directly — it's handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider. Data we do hold (emails, booking details) is stored in an encrypted database with access limited to what's needed to run the service.
